# RoomSlider – Startup Overview for Incubator / Funding Presentation

## 1. Startup Summary
RoomSlider is a digital housing and local services platform built to make the process of finding a home, roommate, and nearby essential services simple, trusted, and fast. The platform is designed for students, working professionals, and families looking for rental housing in Indore and nearby regions.

The website currently includes a wide range of features beyond basic property listing, including:
- Rooms, PGs, hostels, and flats
- Hourly rooms and villa stays
- Laundry and service listings
- Roommate finder and chat
- Social work and donation listings
- Blood request support
- Furniture and vehicle discovery
- Admin dashboards and analytics
- Notifications, user accounts, and direct-owner contact flows

This makes RoomSlider not just a property listing website, but a growing local ecosystem for housing and essential community services.

## 2. Problem We Are Solving
Finding a place to live is a major challenge for students and young professionals. In many cities, the process is still fragmented:
- Listings are scattered across WhatsApp groups, social media, brokers, and local networks
- It is difficult to trust the information shared by strangers
- Searching by area or college requires significant manual effort
- People often cannot compare multiple options quickly
- The process feels stressful, time-consuming, and risky

RoomSlider solves this by turning the experience into an organised, searchable, and trust-focused digital marketplace.

## 3. Our Solution
RoomSlider centralizes the housing discovery journey on one platform.

Key solution elements:
- Verified property discovery by category and area
- Search filters for college/area-based living needs
- Direct contact flow between tenants and owners
- Map-based and category-based browsing
- Rental property detail pages with amenities and contact information
- Lifestyle and service modules such as laundry, villa stays, and roommate matching
- Admin tools for listing management and analytics

The product is built to feel like a complete local housing platform, not just a basic listing site.

## 5. Why This Is a Strong Startup Idea
RoomSlider sits in a large and recurring real-world problem: housing search and local services. This problem exists in every city, with especially high urgency in student hubs and growing urban areas.

What makes this startup promising is that it is not limited to one vertical only:
- Housing discovery is the core business
- Nearby services expand the lifestyle ecosystem
- Community features create stickiness and user retention
- Local trust and direct connections increase adoption in the region

This gives the platform a strong opportunity to become a local marketplace ecosystem rather than a one-feature listing portal.
